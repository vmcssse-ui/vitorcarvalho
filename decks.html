Lesson decks

One PowerPoint per chapter for each of the 5 units (29 decks), generated from the same manifest as the ebook and modules. Every deck follows the four-step model: do-now recall, key words, a read-along, teaching, the same task in three ways (Support, Core, Stretch), an exit ticket and answers. Speaker notes on every slide. Pilot exemplars, awaiting department specialist review.
