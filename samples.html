§ 04Samples

Work you can open

Twenty-seven units in seven subjects — the five core subjects, Geography and History — every book with home-language lines in six languages, the live course portals behind the case study, and the documents. Nothing here asks for an account, and nothing tracks you.

Core-subject exemplars · in build, October 2026

Five exemplars, one model

One unit each in English Language, English Literature, Maths, Combined Science and Triple Science, built from the departments' own lesson decks on the four-step model: a read-along ebook for pre-teaching, then adaptive modules from Support to Stretch that end on the same task. Each is for specialist review before any wider use.

The library · October 2026

Every unit built so far

The same model, unit by unit, from the departments' own schemes of work. Each row opens the unit page, the read-along ebook, the adaptive modules, the key-word arcade and the lesson decks. All are pilot material, awaiting specialist review; home-language lines are on the five exemplars and will follow for the rest.

Home-language lines · EAL

The same book, with a line in the pupil's language

In every read-along, the English stays the text that is read aloud and highlighted; under each sentence a line in the pupil's home language can be switched on, and each key word's meaning appears in that language too. This is the English Together ladder: the home language supports the English rather than replacing it. Open any book below in a language and use the selector at the top of the book to change it. The lines are machine translations and say so on the page until a speaker of that language has signed them off.
